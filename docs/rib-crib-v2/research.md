## Observation → evidence → design implication

1. **Strong red/white identity and recognizable lettering.** Supplied crest, wordmark and lockup establish this directly. Keep these files intact. Use deeper red for navigation and sharing chapters, rather than generic AI-company styling.
2. **English food/social slogans.** The artwork contains “Eat Meat Repeat”, “Let’s Meat Here” and good-food/good-people wording. Proposed copy is friendly and social: “Bring your appetite. And your favourite people.” This is new creative copy, not a historical brand quotation.
3. **A substantial sharing offer.** The original menu names Rib Crib, Barkada, BBQ Ribs and Mega platters. Build a useful platter comparison with actual item names and indicative prices. Rice-cup quantities are transcribed; they are not converted into unsupported guest-serving guarantees.
4. **Rich food and warm interior visual material.** The supplied illustrations suggest an inviting, appetite-led mood. Use large images, warm cream, readable editorial typography and intentional quote/photo layering. Clearly identify atmosphere imagery as illustrative.
5. **Different purchasing questions require different paths.** The menu distinguishes individual specials, wings-only and platters. Keep menu browsing independent of chat; add an optional shortlist and contextual questions. This is a UX inference, not a finding from inaccessible comments.

## Reconciliation and publication decisions

- **Sisig Tacos conflict:** menu-1 lists **₱285** under starters and **₱225** in a featured block on the same image. Neither is silently selected. `price: null` displays “Ask for today’s price”; subtotal excludes it and states that unpriced items remain.
- **Chicken Wings are two different menu options:** six-piece house special **₱245** under the unlimited-rice/iced-tea heading; six wings without rice **₱215** on menu-2. They remain distinct, not conflicting duplicate values.
- **Barbecue Ribs:** the two-piece house special **₱395** is distinct from two ribs without rice **₱325**. The latter includes corn and coleslaw on menu-2.
- **Platters:** ₱875 / ₱1,045 / ₱1,520 / ₱2,225 / ₱2,950 are transcribed. The platter heading states a 1.25L soft drink. No preparation method, available stock or group capacity is invented.
- **Contact:** menu-2 prints **0927 087 4592**. The third-party listing surfaced a different number. Because menu dating and current first-party information could not be confirmed, the website points to the user-specified official Facebook page instead of publishing an unverified telephone number, street address, map pin or opening schedule.
- **Founding:** “EST. 2018” is retained within supplied artwork. No additional founding narrative or ownership history is invented.
- **Menu coverage:** eight illustrated featured dishes, five illustrated platters and additional text-menu groups. Set-meal combinations are not claimed to be exhaustively digitized; the expanded menu directs questions to the restaurant/Jourvis. There is one central data source for displayed prices.
- **Imagery:** all supplied originals stay unchanged. Food and interior visuals are described as illustrative, not proof of actual presentation. No customer faces, reviews, ratings, awards, dietary labels or testimonials were fabricated.
- **Reservations/orders:** enquiry drafts and meal plans are explicitly not confirmed bookings or kitchen orders. No backend/data mutation or external automation was added.
